# Life Goals

Most people do not fail at goals because they lack a list; they fail because the big goals fade from daily awareness and because an inner voice talks them out of acting. Life Goals is a voice-first mobile companion that helps someone write the "ideal scene" of their life, distill it into a small hierarchy of goals (life → 5-year → 1-year → 6-month → month → week), and then keeps those goals alive every day through short, emotionally charged recall and rehearsal moments. The core hypothesis: if a person hears their own future in their own words for a few minutes a day, plans the week from that future, and gets help catching and reframing self-defeating thoughts in the moment, they will act on meaningful goals far more consistently than with any tracker. Differentiation is not storage or tracking; it is recall, meaning, and the inner game.
